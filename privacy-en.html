Privacy policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here is a short explanation of what information reaches us through this site and what we do with it.

What information reaches us

  • When you fill in the contact form (name, phone, neighborhood and interests), the details aren't stored on the site. They become a WhatsApp message that you send us yourself.
  • When you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, we keep the details you gave us in that conversation.

What we do with the information

  • We use it only to get back to you, schedule a consultation, and give you a quote and service.
  • We don't sell, rent or pass it on to anyone.
  • We don't send you advertising messages without your consent.

Cookies and tracking

  • This site uses no cookies and no advertising or tracking tools.
  • Accessibility menu settings are saved only in your browser.
  • We may use an anonymous, cookie-free statistics tool to know how many people visit the site. It doesn't identify you personally.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical data such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

The smart home in your house

The systems we set up run locally in your home, with no cloud and no subscriptions. Remote access for support is given only with your consent, and you can revoke it at any time.

Viewing, correcting and deleting

You can ask to view, correct or delete your information. Contact us on WhatsApp using the button at the top of the page.

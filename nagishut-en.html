Accessibility statement

It matters a great deal to us that everyone, including people with disabilities, can browse this site comfortably and contact us.

What we've done on the site

  • Every page has an accessibility menu (the ♿ button in the corner): larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, stop animations and a readable font. Settings are saved only in your browser.
  • The site is available in Hebrew (right to left) and English, and works on phones, tablets and computers.
  • High color contrast between text and background.
  • You can navigate with the keyboard, and the selected element is marked with a clear outline.
  • You can stop all animations on the site with the "Stop animations" button in the accessibility menu.
  • Illustrations have text descriptions for screen readers.
  • You can enlarge the text with your browser's zoom without breaking the page.

Legal status

The business is a VAT-exempt sole proprietor (osek patur). Under Regulation 35F of Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773–2013, it is currently exempt from the duty to make internet services accessible. Even so, we try to make the site as accessible as possible, and we will keep improving it. The Hebrew statement is the binding version.

Found a problem?

If anything on the site isn't accessible to you, we'd be glad to hear about it and fix it. Contact us on WhatsApp using the button on the home page, and we'll get back to you soon.
